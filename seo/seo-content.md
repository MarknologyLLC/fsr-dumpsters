## FAQ Section - Schema-Ready Q&As

### 1. How much does dumpster rental cost in Kansas City?
Our dumpster rental prices are straightforward: 15-yard dumpsters are $300 per week, and 20-yard dumpsters are $400 per week. This includes delivery, pickup, and disposal fees within our Kansas City Metro service area. No hidden charges or surprise fees.

### 2. What size dumpster do I need for my project?
A 15-yard dumpster works well for small to medium home cleanouts, garage cleanouts, small remodeling projects, and minor landscaping debris. A 20-yard dumpster is ideal for larger home renovations, roofing projects, major cleanouts, and construction debris. If you're unsure, call us at (913) 206-3467 for personalized recommendations.

### 3. How long can I keep the dumpster?
Our standard rental period is 7 days. This gives you plenty of time to complete your project without feeling rushed. If you need the dumpster longer, contact us to discuss extended rental options.

### 4. What areas do you serve around Kansas City?
We serve the entire Kansas City Metro area across 7 counties: Clay, Jackson, Platte, and Cass counties in Missouri, plus Johnson, Wyandotte, and Leavenworth counties in Kansas. This includes Kansas City, Gladstone, Liberty, Independence, Overland Park, Olathe, Lenexa, Lee's Summit, Blue Springs, and surrounding communities.

### 5. How quickly can you deliver a dumpster?
We offer same-day delivery when you call before noon, Monday through Friday. For weekend delivery, please call by Thursday to ensure availability. Our goal is to get you the dumpster when you need it.

### 6. What can I put in the dumpster?
You can dispose of most household debris, construction materials, furniture, appliances (without refrigerants), yard waste, and general junk. We cannot accept hazardous materials, electronics, batteries, paint, chemicals, or tires. Contact us if you have questions about specific items.

### 7. Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Kansas City?
Generally, no permit is required if the dumpster is placed on your private property like a driveway. If you need to place it on a city street or public right-of-way, you may need a permit from your local municipality. We can help guide you through this process.

### 8. How much space do I need for dumpster placement?
You'll need a flat, stable surface approximately 60 feet long by 10.5 feet wide for delivery truck access. This includes space for the dumpster (about 22 feet long) and room for our delivery truck to maneuver safely.

### 9. Can you deliver to construction sites?
Absolutely! We regularly deliver to construction and renovation sites throughout the Kansas City Metro. Our drivers are experienced with jobsite deliveries and can work around your project timeline.

### 10. What makes FSR Dumpsters different from other companies?
We're locally owned and operated in Gladstone, Missouri, with deep roots in the Kansas City community. We provide transparent pricing, reliable service, and personal attention to every customer. No corporate bureaucracy - just honest, dependable dumpster rental service.

### 11. Do you offer commercial dumpster services?
Yes, we serve both residential and commercial customers throughout the Kansas City Metro. Whether you're a contractor, business owner, or property manager, we can accommodate your waste disposal needs with flexible scheduling and competitive pricing.

### 12. What happens if I fill the dumpster beyond capacity?
For safety reasons, dumpsters cannot be overfilled. Materials should not extend above the top edge of the container. If your dumpster is overfilled, we may need to remove excess material before pickup, which could result in additional charges.
