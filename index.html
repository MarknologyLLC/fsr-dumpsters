Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our dumpster rental service

How much does dumpster rental cost in Kansas City?

Our dumpster rental prices are straightforward: 15-yard dumpsters are $300 per week, and 20-yard dumpsters are $400 per week. This includes delivery, pickup, and disposal fees within our Kansas City Metro service area. No hidden charges or surprise fees.

What size dumpster do I need for my project?

A 15-yard dumpster works well for small to medium home cleanouts, garage cleanouts, small remodeling projects, and minor landscaping debris. A 20-yard dumpster is ideal for larger home renovations, roofing projects, major cleanouts, and construction debris. If you're unsure, call us at (913) 206-3467 for personalized recommendations.

How long can I keep the dumpster?

Our standard rental period is 7 days. This gives you plenty of time to complete your project without feeling rushed. If you need the dumpster longer, contact us to discuss extended rental options.

What can I put in the dumpster?

You can dispose of most household junk, construction debris, furniture, appliances, yard waste, and general trash. We cannot accept hazardous materials, chemicals, paint, batteries, tires, or liquids. Contact us if you have questions about specific items.

How quickly can you deliver a dumpster?

We offer same-day delivery when you call before noon, Monday through Friday. For weekend delivery, please call by Thursday to ensure availability. Our goal is to get you the dumpster when you need it.

Where will the dumpster be placed on my property?

We can place the dumpster in your driveway, on the street (if permitted), or anywhere on your property that our truck can safely access. We recommend a flat, hard surface and clear path for delivery. We'll work with you to find the best placement.

Do I need a permit for a dumpster?

If the dumpster is placed on your property (driveway, yard), no permit is typically required. If placed on a city street or right-of-way, you may need a permit from your local municipality. We can help advise you on local requirements.

What are the weight limits?

15-yard dumpsters have a weight limit of 3 tons, and 20-yard dumpsters have a weight limit of 4 tons. Heavy materials like concrete, dirt, and roofing shingles can add up quickly. If you're disposing of heavy materials, let us know when booking.
