How It Works

From your first call to final pickup. Simple and straightforward.

1

Step One

Call or Text to Book

No online forms to wrestle with. Just call or text contact us and tell us:

  • Which size dumpster you need (13, 17, or 21-yard)
  • Your delivery address
  • Your preferred delivery date and time window
  • Any tight access notes (narrow driveway, gate code, etc.)

We'll confirm availability, walk you through the price, and lock in your booking. Usually in under 5 minutes.

Same-day delivery? Yes, subject to schedule availability. Call early, and we'll do our best to make it happen.
2

Step Two

We Deliver and Set It Up

Our truck arrives in your scheduled window. We place the dumpster exactly where you need it, whether that's your driveway, your side yard, or that tight gangway between your house and your neighbor's fence.

  • a

    Wooden boards go down first. Every single delivery, every single time. We protect your concrete or asphalt. no extra charge.

  • b

    We position for your workflow. Tell us which end you want accessible. We'll set the dumpster doors or opening at the right angle for how you're loading.

  • c

    We brief you on loading rules. Quick walkthrough: what goes in, what doesn't, the fill line. Takes 60 seconds.

3

Step Three

Fill It Up (7 Days)

You've got 7 days from delivery. Load at your own pace. The dumpster is yours for the whole rental period.

Load debris level with or below the top rail. Nothing can stick up above the rim (safety and transport rules).

Don't overfill above the sidewalls. Overfilled dumpsters can't be legally transported and you'll need to remove excess debris.

Need more time? Call us to extend at $20/day. Need to swap for a fresh dumpster mid-project? We do that too ($200 flat).

See the full list of what you can and can't put in →

4

Step Four

We Pick It Up

When you're done. or at the end of your 7-day rental. Just call us and we'll schedule pickup. We haul the dumpster to a licensed disposal facility and handle all the dumping.

  • Driveway boards removed, your surface left clean
  • No leftover mess, no debris left behind
  • Responsible disposal at licensed facilities

If you went over the included weight allowance, you'll receive a call with the overage amount. No surprises. We walk through it transparently.
