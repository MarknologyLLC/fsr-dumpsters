Common Questions About KC Dumpster Rental

How much does a dumpster rental cost in Kansas City?

Every rental includes delivery, 7 days, free driveway board protection, a base weight allowance, and pickup. No hidden fuel surcharges. Call us at (404) 759-4361 for a same-day quote on any size. Extensions are $20/day and weight overages are $75/ton above the included limit.

What size dumpster do I need?

13-yard for small cleanouts and single-room renos. 17-yard for whole-home cleanouts and multi-room renovations (most popular). 21-yard for roofing, large construction, and full property cleanouts. All three share the same 14x8 ft driveway footprint, so you pick by volume, not space.

Do I need a permit for a dumpster in Kansas City?

No permit needed for driveway or private property placement. If you need street or sidewalk placement, you will need to obtain a right-of-way permit from KC Public Works or your local municipality before our truck arrives. We do not pull permits on your behalf.

Can I get same-day delivery near Kansas City?

Yes, subject to schedule availability. Call as early as possible, mornings are best. We prefer 24 hours of notice but regularly accommodate same-day requests throughout the Greater KC metro, including Overland Park, Independence, Lee's Summit, and Kansas City proper.
