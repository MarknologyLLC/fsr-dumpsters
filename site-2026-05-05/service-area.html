35-Mile Radius from KC Metro

We operate from our base in the Greater Kansas City metro and cover approximately 35 miles in all directions, reaching communities throughout the Missouri and Kansas sides of the metro.

For deliveries 36-50 miles out, we charge a $100 flat out-of-area surcharge. Beyond 50 miles, we typically decline. But call us, because there are exceptions.

Planning to Put the Dumpster on the Street?

If your dumpster needs to go on a public street or sidewalk rather than your private driveway, you'll need to obtain a right-of-way permit from your municipality before our truck arrives.

  • Kansas City, MO: Kansas City Public Works. (816) 513-2600
  • Overland Park / Johnson County KS: Contact your city public works department
  • Other cities: Contact your city's public works or code enforcement department

We don't pull permits on your behalf. If a permit isn't in place when our truck arrives and we can't place the dumpster, a $100 failed-trip fee applies.
