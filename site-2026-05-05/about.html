About Kansas City
Dumpster Rentals

Locally Owned

KC People. KC Service. KC Know-How.

Kansas City Dumpster Rentals was built to solve a simple problem: the KC metro deserves a locally owned roll-off dumpster company that actually knows the territory, shows up on time, and treats your property with respect.

We're not a franchise. We're not a call center in another state routing your order to whoever answers the phone. When you call us, you're talking to the operation. We know these streets. We know which Midtown gangways are tight. We know where to position the dumpster so your truck can still get in and out.

We started with one commitment: every delivery gets driveway boards. Every one. No extra charge, no asking. Because your driveway matters and we're going to treat it that way. Everything else follows from that.

Our Equipment

Professional Rig, Professional Results

We run a White Ford Super Duty F-Series FX4 paired with a Texas Pride gooseneck dump trailer. That's a commercial-grade setup built for reliability and precise placement, not a patched-together fleet.

Our dumpsters are clean, modern black roll-off containers. 13-yard, 17-yard, and 21-yard. All with the same 14x8 ft footprint so they fit in standard driveways. All delivered with wooden boards underneath, every time.
