The Same-Footprint Advantage

All three of our dumpsters share the same 14×8 ft driveway footprint. The only difference is height: 4 ft for the 13-yard, 5 ft for the 17-yard, 6 ft for the 21-yard. That means if a dumpster fits in your driveway, all three sizes fit. You choose based on how much debris you have, not how much space you have.

Street or Sidewalk Placement: Permit Required

If your dumpster needs to go on a public street or sidewalk (rather than your private driveway), you're responsible for obtaining a right-of-way permit from Kansas City Public Works (or your municipality) before our truck arrives. We do not handle permitting. Need help knowing who to contact? Call us and we'll point you in the right direction.
