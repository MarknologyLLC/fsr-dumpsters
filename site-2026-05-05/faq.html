Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you rent.

Sizing

Here's the quick guide:

  • 13-yard: Small garage cleanout, single-room renovation, small landscaping job, minor junk removal.
  • 17-yard: Whole-home cleanout, multi-room renovation, deck or fence removal, estate cleanout. This is the most popular size.
  • 21-yard: Roofing jobs, large construction projects, full property cleanouts where you're generating a high volume of debris.

All three sizes share the same 14×8 ft driveway footprint. Only the height changes. So driveway space isn't really a factor in which you choose. Still not sure? Call us and we'll nail it down in 2 minutes. contact us

Yes. All three sizes share the same 14-foot length and 8-foot width. The only thing that changes is the height: 4 ft for the 13-yard, 5 ft for the 17-yard, 6 ft for the 21-yard. If your driveway can accommodate a 14×8 ft space, it can accommodate any of our dumpsters.

When in doubt, size up. A dumpster that's too small means you're either overfilling (not allowed) or calling for a swap-out, which costs time and money. Going one size larger rarely costs much more and saves the headache.

We can also do a swap-out if you fill up and need a fresh dumpster. $200 flat for a dump-and-return. Call us and we'll advise based on what you describe.
