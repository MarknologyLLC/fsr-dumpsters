Construction Debris Disposal in KC: A Contractor's Quick Guide

If you are running construction or remodeling projects in Kansas City, dumpster service is a recurring cost worth optimizing. This is the contractor-focused guide. Repeat-use pricing, the swap-out service that keeps your jobsite clean, weight management strategies, and what fits in our 21-yard.

Published May 5, 2026 · Locally written by Kansas City Dumpster Rentals

Contractors are the ideal dumpster customers. You know what you need, you know how big to size it, and you turn it over fast. We work with KC area remodel, construction, and property management contractors regularly. Here is everything you need to know to make our service work for your jobs.

The Swap-Out Service

The most useful tool in your toolbox for active jobsites: the swap-out.

How it works:

  1. You fill the dumpster on your jobsite
  2. You call or text us when it is full
  3. We come to the jobsite, pick up the full dumpster, drop a fresh empty one in the same spot
  4. You keep working, no downtime
  5. We dispose of the full one separately

Cost: $200 flat per swap, plus the original tonnage allowance applies to the fresh dumpster. If the swap load was over the included weight, $75 per ton overage applies.

For active multi-week jobs, swap-outs are cheaper than ordering a new full rental each time. They also keep your jobsite clean without you having to schedule pickups around the project schedule.

Weight Management

The thing that gets contractors in trouble on dumpster billing is weight overages. Here is how to keep them in check:

Heavy materials to limit (or pre-sort)

  • Concrete: If you have small chunks (under a few hundred pounds), it is fine in a mixed load. If you are demoing a sidewalk or driveway, do not put it in a regular dumpster. Concrete-specific containers are available from specialty providers in KC.
  • Brick and masonry: Same rule. Small quantities OK, larger volumes need a specialty container.
  • Dirt and gravel: Heavy as concrete. Use a clean-fill site (free dirt drop-off in some KC suburbs) or a specialty container.
  • Roofing shingles: Not accepted in our standard dumpsters. Use a specialty roofing container (see our roofing dumpster guide).
  • Plaster, mortar, tile in volume: Heavy. Call us before loading large amounts.

Light bulky materials that fill volume but stay under tonnage

  • Drywall (cut into 4 ft pieces and stacked, drywall packs efficiently and is light)
  • Wood framing scraps (light, packs flat)
  • Insulation (very light, fills volume fast)
  • Vinyl flooring, carpet, padding
  • Cabinet boxes, fixtures, paneling

The pro move

If your project has a mix of heavy and light, load light materials first to fill volume, then top off with heavy materials within the tonnage allowance. You get more bang for the buck without overage fees.

What We Accept (Construction Debris List)

Standard construction and remodel debris welcome:

  • Drywall, plaster (light volumes)
  • Lumber, plywood, OSB, framing scraps
  • Wood and laminate flooring, vinyl flooring, carpet, padding
  • Demolition debris (mixed)
  • Cabinets, vanities, countertops (laminate or solid surface)
  • Light fixtures, plumbing fixtures (sinks, toilets - drained)
  • Doors, windows (glass intact)
  • Insulation
  • Trim, baseboard, crown molding
  • Light demolition metal (small flashing pieces)

What We Do Not Accept

  • Concrete in volume (small chunks OK)
  • Brick and masonry in volume
  • Roofing shingles
  • Hazardous materials: paint cans (unless empty and dry), solvents, chemicals, asbestos
  • Tires
  • Batteries (any kind)
  • Refrigerators with freon, AC units
  • Electronics (TVs, monitors)
  • Medical waste

If you have specialty waste, call us. We can usually point you to a KC provider that handles it correctly.

Scheduling for Active Jobs

Most contractor jobs benefit from a 7 or 14-day rental window:

  • 7-day: One-week renovation projects (kitchen, bath, multi-room)
  • 14-day: Two-week projects, additions, larger demolition phases
  • Swap-outs: Multi-week projects where you need continuous disposal capacity

If your project length is uncertain, start with a 7-day and extend at $20/day if needed. Cheaper than over-committing to a 14-day on a project that finishes in 9 days.

Same-Day and Next-Day Delivery

For active jobsites, schedule changes are constant. We accommodate:

  • Same-day delivery: Call before noon, available based on schedule
  • Next-day delivery: Call any time, very high availability
  • Late-afternoon pickup: Available, just text us when you are done

Driveway Boards on Every Drop

Even for jobsites, we put wooden boards under every dumpster. Concrete driveways scratch, asphalt softens in summer heat, and the trailer rails can leave marks. Free protection, no upcharge, every drop. The few extra minutes on each delivery save real money in driveway repair down the road.

The contractor takeaway: The 21-yard dumpster is the right size for most full-room remodels and gut jobs. Match weight to material (load light bulk first, heavy second). Use the $200 swap-out for active multi-week jobs. Volume-based contractor pricing available, just ask.
