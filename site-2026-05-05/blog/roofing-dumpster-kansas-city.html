Roofing Project in Kansas City? Why a Standard Dumpster Won't Work

If you are doing a roofing tear-off in Kansas City, a standard residential dumpster is not the right tool. Shingles are heavy, regulated, and most roll-off providers (us included) do not accept them in mixed loads. Here is the why, the alternatives, and the providers in KC who specialize in roofing waste.

Published May 5, 2026 · Locally written by Kansas City Dumpster Rentals

If you are doing a roofing tear-off on your Kansas City home, do not call us. We are going to refer you out, and here is why.

Standard residential roll-off dumpsters (the 13, 17, and 21-yard sizes most KC providers run) are not built for roofing waste. The reasons are weight, disposal regulations, and economics. Most local providers (Alpha Team KC, FSR Dumpsters, Crown Dumpster, us) explicitly exclude roofing shingles from our service or handle them as a specialty offering with different pricing.

This article walks you through why, what to do instead, and which KC providers actually handle roofing waste correctly.

Why Shingles Break Standard Dumpsters

1. Weight

Asphalt shingles weigh approximately 3 to 4 pounds per square foot. A 2,000 square foot single-layer roof tear-off generates 6,000 to 8,000 lbs of debris. That is 3 to 4 tons of weight.

Our standard residential dumpsters include 1.5 to 2.5 tons of disposal weight. A residential roof tear-off would blow through that allowance immediately, generating $300 to $400 in overage fees on top of the rental price.

For multi-layer tear-offs (where the contractor strips two or three layers of old shingles), the weight doubles or triples. Now you are at 9,000 to 16,000 lbs of waste, far beyond what a standard residential dumpster is designed to handle.

2. Landfill regulations

Many landfills in the KC metro and surrounding counties have specific rules for asphalt shingles. Some accept them at higher tipping fees. Some require shingles to be separated from other waste. Some only take them on certain days.

Standard residential dumpster providers do not specialize in this routing. Specialty roofing dumpster operators do. They have direct relationships with landfills that accept roofing waste and they pre-route their loads accordingly.

3. Insurance and liability

Tear-off debris often contains nails, broken flashing, and metal sheeting. These create injury risks for our team during pickup. Specialty roofing operators have crews trained for this work and insurance underwriters who understand the risk profile.

What to Do Instead in Kansas City

Option 1: Hire a roofing contractor that handles disposal

Most professional roofing contractors in Kansas City include debris removal in their bid. They tear off the old roof, install the new one, and haul away the waste. Total project price typically reflects this. If you are working with a roofing contractor, ask if disposal is included before they start. Most reputable KC roofers handle this without you having to think about it.

Option 2: Specialty roofing dumpster

If you are doing a DIY roof tear-off (rare for residential, more common for outbuildings, sheds, garages, decks with shingled roofs), you need a specialty container. Several KC providers offer them:

  • Alpha Team KC: Has indicated they offer concrete and specialty containers in addition to standard sizes
  • Crown Dumpster: Multi-size offering, can quote roofing specifically
  • Local roofing supply houses: Sometimes partner with disposal companies for tear-off projects
  • National providers (Waste Management, Republic Services): Higher priced, but they have specialty equipment routes

Call ahead, specifically ask: "I am doing a residential roof tear-off, what is your roofing dumpster pricing and what is included?"

Option 3: Bag-based haul-away (small jobs only)

For very small roofing jobs (a 200 to 400 sqft shed roof, a single-section repair), some KC homeowners bag the shingles in heavy-duty contractor bags and use the regular trash + bulk pickup service. This works for small volumes only. A full house tear-off generates far too much waste for this approach.

What a Roofing Dumpster Actually Costs in KC

For specialty roofing containers in the Kansas City metro:

  • 15 to 20-yard specialty roofing dumpster, residential single-layer tear-off: $400 to $700
  • 30-yard specialty roofing dumpster, residential multi-layer or larger roof: $700 to $1,000
  • 40-yard or larger commercial roofing project: $1,000 to $2,000+

The price reflects:

  • Higher disposal fees at landfills that accept shingles
  • Specialized equipment for handling heavy loads
  • Routing to specific facilities

What Goes in a Roofing Dumpster (and What Does Not)

A specialty roofing dumpster typically accepts:

  • Asphalt shingles (3-tab, architectural, designer)
  • Roofing felt and underlayment
  • Drip edge and flashing (metal, in moderate quantities)
  • Roofing nails and small fasteners
  • Old plywood decking (sometimes; ask the provider)

What you cannot put in a roofing dumpster:

  • Asbestos-containing roofing materials (older homes built before 1980 may have these; requires hazardous abatement)
  • Wet or moldy organic waste
  • General household trash mixed in
  • Ceramic or clay tile (different specialty container)

The Asbestos Question (Important for KC Older Homes)

Kansas City has a lot of pre-1980 housing stock, especially in Brookside, Hyde Park, Westport, and similar neighborhoods. Some of these homes have asbestos-containing roofing materials underneath the visible shingles.

If your home was built before 1980 and you are doing a tear-off, get an asbestos test before you start. KC has licensed asbestos abatement contractors who can test and, if needed, handle the abatement portion of the project. The cost is $200 to $500 for testing, and abatement (if needed) is a significant added expense, but it is critical for safety and legal compliance.

Why We Are Telling You This (Even Though It Is Not Our Service)

Most local Kansas City dumpster providers either accept roofing waste reluctantly with high overage fees or simply turn people away without explaining why. We chose the third path: tell you upfront we do not handle this, explain why, and point you to who does.

The honest reason: we would rather you have a great experience with the right provider for your specific project than try to force our service to fit a need it is not built for.

The takeaway: Roofing tear-offs in Kansas City need specialty containers, not standard residential dumpsters. Most KC homeowners get this handled by their roofing contractor as part of the project bid. If you are DIY tear-off, expect $400 to $700 for a typical specialty roofing dumpster. We do not offer this service, but Alpha Team KC, Crown Dumpster, and several other local providers do.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I put roofing shingles in a regular dumpster in Kansas City?

Most KC roll-off providers (including us) do not accept roofing shingles in standard residential dumpsters. Shingles are heavy and regulated. You need a specialty roofing dumpster.

How much does a roofing dumpster cost in Kansas City?

Specialty roofing dumpsters in the KC metro run $400 to $800 for a typical residential tear-off. Larger commercial projects can be $1,000+.

What size dumpster do I need for a roof tear-off?

For a typical residential single-layer tear-off (1,500 to 2,500 sqft), a 15 or 20-yard specialty roofing container fits. Multi-layer or larger roofs need 30-yard or larger.

Doing a Different Project?

If you are doing a remodel or general construction project (not roofing), our standard dumpsters work great. Request a quote with the project type and we will recommend the right size or refer you to a specialty provider if your project needs it.
