Garage Cleanout in Kansas City: A Step-by-Step Guide

A garage cleanout is one of those projects that lives on your weekend list for years until you finally tackle it. This is the practical guide. Sorting strategy, real KC disposal options, the math on DIY trips to the dump versus renting a dumpster, and the right-sized dumpster if you go that route.

Published May 5, 2026 · Locally written by Kansas City Dumpster Rentals

A garage cleanout is the project that lives at the bottom of your home maintenance list. The boxes from the move three years ago, the leftover paint cans from the kitchen remodel, the kids' bikes they outgrew, the holiday decorations you replaced last December: it all stacks up.

This is the honest, practical guide for KC homeowners on how to do it. Includes the math on whether to rent a dumpster or do trips to the dump yourself.

Phase 1: Sort First, Then Decide What to Throw

Block a Saturday morning. Pull everything out into the driveway. Sort into 5 piles:

  1. Keep: Things you actually use, with a clear home back in the garage.
  2. Donate: Working items in good condition (toys, sports gear, working tools, kitchenware that migrated to the garage).
  3. Sell: Higher-value items (kids' bikes worth $50+, old furniture, tools you no longer need).
  4. Hazardous: Paint, oil, batteries, chemicals, fluorescent bulbs, automotive fluids. Cannot go in regular trash.
  5. Trash: Everything else.

The trash pile is what determines how big a dumpster you need (or how many trips to the dump).

Phase 2: Where Each Pile Actually Goes

Donate (KC options)

  • Goodwill: Multiple drop-off locations across the metro. Easy for clothing, kitchenware, decor, working small appliances.
  • Salvation Army: Bannister Road location accepts furniture, household goods. Pick-up service for larger items.
  • Habitat for Humanity ReStore: Multiple KC area locations. Building materials, working appliances, furniture, fixtures. They will pick up larger items.
  • City Union Mission: Clothing, household goods.

Sell

  • Facebook Marketplace: highest reach for KC, fastest sales
  • Local KC garage sale groups on Facebook
  • OfferUp, Craigslist KC for higher-value items
  • Plato's Closet, 2nd Avenue for clothing in good condition

Hazardous waste

Do not put any of this in a dumpster: paint cans (wet or dry), motor oil, antifreeze, household chemicals, fluorescent bulbs, automotive fluids, propane tanks, batteries, pesticides, fireworks.

KC has a free Household Hazardous Waste drop-off program for residents. Check kcmo.gov for current locations and hours. Most KC residents can drop off up to 25 gallons or 250 lbs of hazardous waste per visit at no charge.

Electronics

  • Surplus Exchange (KCK): accepts most electronics, some donations
  • Best Buy: small electronics drop-off (free for most items)
  • Staples: similar small electronics recycling
  • Goodwill: working electronics in donation

Trash (the part this guide is about)

Two paths: trips to the dump in your truck, or renting a roll-off dumpster.

The Math: Truck Trips vs. Dumpster Rental

Most KC homeowners debate this. Here is the honest comparison for a typical garage cleanout:

Path A: Trips to the Bannister Transfer Station in Your Truck

  • Truck capacity: 1.5 to 2 cubic yards per load (a typical pickup truck bed)
  • Garage cleanout volume: Most KC two-car garage cleanouts produce 6 to 12 cubic yards of trash. That is 4 to 8 truck trips.
  • Tipping fee at Bannister: $30 to $80 per load, depending on weight
  • Gas: Roughly $5 to $10 per round trip from a typical KC residential address
  • Time: 1 to 2 hours per round trip including loading, driving, unloading, driving home
  • Total cost (4-8 trips): $140 to $720 in tipping + $20 to $80 in gas = $160 to $800
  • Total time: 4 to 16 hours of your weekends

Path B: Roll-Off Dumpster on Your Driveway

  • 13-yard dumpster, 3-day rental: $300 (1.5 tons included)
  • 17-yard dumpster, 7-day rental: $400 (2 tons included)
  • Time: Load it on your schedule, we pick up. No driving.
  • Total cost: $300 to $400 for most garage cleanouts
  • Total time: 0 hours of driving, all loading happens at home

Verdict

Truck trips win on cost only if you are doing a small cleanout (1 to 3 truck loads worth) and your time is essentially free. For most KC garage cleanouts, the dumpster rental costs about the same or less than the dump trips, and you save 4 to 16 hours.

The dumpster also wins on family stress: nobody wants to spend 4 weekends driving to the transfer station. One weekend, one dumpster, done.

What Size Dumpster for a Garage Cleanout

  • Small single-car garage, light cleanout: 13-yard ($300 for 3 days)
  • Two-car garage, normal accumulation: 17-yard ($350 for 3 days, $400 for 7 days)
  • Two-car garage plus basement, attic, or yard debris combined: 21-yard ($400 for 3 days, $450 for 7 days)

If you are doing the garage at the same time as another project (kitchen remodel, basement cleanout), bundle them. One bigger dumpster is cheaper than two smaller ones.

The Realistic Timeline

A typical 2-car garage cleanout in Kansas City fits in one weekend if you are organized:

  • Friday evening: Sort the garage, pull everything out into the driveway. (3 hours)
  • Saturday morning: Load donations into your car, drop off at Goodwill, Salvation Army, ReStore. Take hazardous waste to KC drop-off. (3 hours)
  • Saturday afternoon: Load the dumpster. (2 to 4 hours)
  • Sunday: Reorganize what you kept, mount shelving, hang tools, take a long deserved break. (3 to 6 hours)
  • Monday: We pick up the dumpster. Garage is done.

Tools That Make This Easier

  • Heavy-duty contractor trash bags (3 mil minimum)
  • A good pair of work gloves
  • A box cutter and a pair of tin snips
  • A handcart or dolly for heavy items
  • Sharpie + masking tape for labeling boxes during the sort

The honest takeaway: A roll-off dumpster usually beats truck trips on cost AND time for any garage cleanout that produces more than 2-3 truck loads of trash. Most KC two-car garage cleanouts fit a 17-yard dumpster, $400 for a 7-day rental, and one organized weekend.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a garage cleanout cost in Kansas City?

A typical KC garage cleanout costs $300 to $400 in dumpster rental fees if you DIY (13 or 17-yard dumpster, 3 to 7 day rental). DIY trips to the dump in your truck can run $160 to $800 plus 4 to 16 hours of weekends.

What size dumpster do I need for a garage cleanout?

Single-car garage cleanout: 13-yard. Two-car garage with normal accumulation: 17-yard. Two-car garage plus other projects (basement, attic, yard): 21-yard.

Where can I dump garage debris in Kansas City?

KC Public Works operates the Bannister Transfer Station for residential drop-off. Tipping fees $30 to $80 per load. Hazardous waste goes to the Household Hazardous Waste facility separately, free for KC residents.
