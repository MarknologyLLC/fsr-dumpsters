What Size Dumpster Do I Need? A Kansas City Homeowner's Guide

The right dumpster size for your project depends on debris volume and weight, not driveway space. All three of our sizes have the same 14-foot by 8-foot footprint, so if a 13-yard fits, a 21-yard fits too. Pick by what you are throwing away.

Published May 5, 2026 · Locally written by Kansas City Dumpster Rentals

Picking the wrong size dumpster is one of the most common Kansas City rental mistakes. Go too small and you end up with overage fees or needing a second dumpster. Go too big and you pay for capacity you never used. We are going to walk through this the way we would if you called us on the phone: by project, with real-world examples, in plain English.

Match by Project, Not by Volume Guess

Here is the cleanest way to pick the right size: tell us what the project is. We have done enough KC residential cleanouts to estimate volume from project type alone. Match yours to one of these:

13-Yard Projects

  • Single-room cleanout (one bedroom, one home office)
  • Bathroom remodel (tile, fixtures, vanity, flooring)
  • Yard waste cleanup (one or two trees, a few weekends of weeding and pruning)
  • Light decluttering (closets, basement boxes, attic items)
  • Single-car garage cleanout

17-Yard Projects

  • Multi-room cleanout (two to three bedrooms, plus storage areas)
  • Kitchen remodel (cabinets, countertops, flooring, appliances)
  • Two-car garage cleanout
  • Basement cleanout (small to medium)
  • Mid-size landscaping projects (sod removal, deck demo)
  • Roofing tear-off for a small section (note: full roofing projects need specialty containers, see below)

21-Yard Projects

  • Full home cleanout (estate, move-out, hoarder-adjacent situations)
  • Large remodel (multi-room renovation, addition, gut job)
  • Construction debris from a moderate building project
  • Rental property turnover (full unit clear-out)
  • Storm cleanup (large-scale yard and structure debris)

The Tonnage Test

Volume tells you how much fits. Tonnage tells you how much you can throw away before overage fees hit. These are different.

You hit your tonnage allowance fast with:

  • Heavy debris: Concrete, brick, dirt, gravel, sand, asphalt, roofing shingles. We do not accept most of these in standard dumpsters anyway, but mixed loads with these materials add weight fast.
  • Furniture and appliances at scale: A full bedroom set, a refrigerator (without freon), a couch, a dining set, a deck of patio furniture: that easily adds 800+ lbs.
  • Wet yard debris: Wet sod, wet leaves, wet wood. Water adds weight you do not see until the load is on the scale.

You stay light on tonnage with:

  • Boxes, paper, cardboard, fabric
  • Drywall and lath (less than you think)
  • Light construction debris like 2x4 framing scrap, vinyl flooring
  • Plastics, toys, decor, household clutter

Rule of thumb: if your project is volume-heavy but light, size up. If it is compact but heavy, size down or accept the overage as part of the budget.

Will It Fit My Driveway?

Yes, almost certainly. The footprint of every dumpster we run is 14 ft x 8 ft. That is roughly the size of a single-car parking space. If you can fit a car comfortably on your driveway, you can fit any of our dumpsters there.

The exceptions in Kansas City are the tightest Midtown driveways: Brookside, Westport, Hyde Park, Waldo neighborhoods with narrow gangways between houses. The "Same Footprint" Insight

This is the thing most KC homeowners do not realize: going bigger does not require more driveway space. The 13-yard, 17-yard, and 21-yard all have the same 14 x 8 ft footprint. They just go up. So when you are debating between sizes, the question is not "do I have room for the bigger one?" The answer is yes. The real question is "how much am I throwing away?"

The takeaway: Pick by debris volume, not driveway space. If you cannot decide between two sizes, go up one. Overage fees from undersizing usually cost more than the price difference between sizes.

Frequently Asked Questions

What size dumpster do I need for a single garage cleanout in Kansas City?

For a typical residential one or two-car garage cleanout, a 13-yard dumpster (1.5 tons included) handles most situations. If you are also clearing yard debris, a basement, or doing multi-room work, step up to a 17-yard.

What size dumpster fits a typical KC driveway?

All three sizes (13, 17, and 21-yard) have the same 14 ft by 8 ft footprint. Only the height changes. If your driveway fits a parked car, it fits any of our dumpsters.

What size dumpster do I need for a full home cleanout?

For a full residential home cleanout in the KC area, the 21-yard is the starting point. It handles 2.5 tons of mixed household debris and furniture from a typical 2,000 to 3,000 sqft home in one rental. Larger projects may need a swap-out (we empty it and bring it back) for $200.

Can I upsize after delivery?

Honest answer: not really. Once a smaller dumpster is on your driveway, the cleanest path is to fill it, have us pick it up, and order another one. Better to size up at the start, even if you are not sure.
